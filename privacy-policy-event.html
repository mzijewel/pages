EventManager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

EventManager helps groups record and split shared expenses. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The app is published by <Zahidul Islam Jewel> (we).

Using the app as a guest

If you choose Continue offline as guest, everything you enter (events, member names, expenses, funds and descriptions) stays in a database on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional and is used only to back up your events and share them with other users. When you sign in:

  • Account data: we receive your Google account name, email address and an account ID from Google, through our backend provider Supabase.
  • Event data: events, member names, expense and fund amounts, descriptions and dates you create are stored on our Supabase servers so they can be synced to your devices.
  • Sharing you control: if you add another registered user to an event, that user can see that event's data. Managers you choose can also edit it.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access on the server is limited by per-user security rules.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.
  • We don't access your contacts, location, camera or photos.

Service providers

We use Supabase to host the sync database and sign-in, and Google Sign-In to verify your identity. They process data only to provide those services.

Backups you export

The Export data option creates a file on your phone and opens the share sheet. Where that file goes is up to you. We never receive it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep synced data while your account exists. Signing out removes the synced data from your phone. To delete your account and all its data from our servers, use Delete account in the app's Profile screen, or email us at the address below. We will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date above.
